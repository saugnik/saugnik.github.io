Our Story

Where Every Meal Feels Royal

At Rajasik Restaurant & Banquet, dining is a celebration. Nestled in the heart of Rishra, we bring together the bold spices of North India, the fragrant smoke of the tandoor, and the wok-fired magic of the Far East — all crafted from the finest ingredients and time-honoured recipes.

Whether it's a quiet family dinner, a Friday night out with live music, or a grand wedding in our spacious banquet hall, our table is always set for you.

  • Freshly prepared, authentic recipes
  • Warm, family-friendly ambience
  • Dine-in, takeaway & home delivery
  • Air-conditioned banquet for 200+ guests
